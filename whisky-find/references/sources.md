**The market is not the bottler.** A retailer's, importer's, or festival's
selection of an official bottling is not an independent bottling: the producer
stays the brand, there is no bottler, and the program name is an edition. But
its reviews live in the destination market's language. Route on both.

| Pattern | Search |
|---|---|
| Scotch distillery, Japan-exclusive release | en + ja |
| Scotch distillery, Taiwan-exclusive release | en + zh |
| Scotch distillery, Korean importer selection | en + ko |
| Scotch distillery, German retailer cask | en + de |
| Japanese producer, European release | ja + en |

## Facts and reviews

Read facts and opinions from different places. Never read a forum for an ABV.

**Facts** — cask type, ABV, outturn, vintage, dates:
the bottler's own site and the label itself, then Whiskybase, then retailer
listings, then auction lot descriptions.

**Reviews and scores, by language:**

| Lang | Where |
|---|---|
| en | Whiskybase ratings, Whiskyfun, MALT Review, Whisky Notes, Dramface, Words of Whisky, WhiskyCast, r/Scotch |

## Auction

**Whiskyhunter.net first.** It aggregates results across roughly twenty auction
houses, so one fetch replaces twenty. **Whiskystats** for price index and trend.
Go to an individual house only when Whiskyhunter has no record of the bottling.

| Region | Houses |
|---|---|
| UK | Whisky Auctioneer, Scotch Whisky Auctions, Whisky Hammer, Just Whisky, Whisky.Auction |
| EU | Catawiki, whiskyauction.com |
| JP | ヤフオク (Yahoo! Auctions), Est-Ouest, Shinwa |
| Asia, high end | Bonhams Hong Kong, Sotheby's Hong Kong |
| Fine and rare, global | Sotheby's, Christie's, Bonhams |

**Buyer's premium.** A hammer price is not what the buyer paid. Most houses add
a premium, commonly around 10%, sometimes plus VAT and shipping. Rates differ by
house and change; read the house's own terms rather than assuming 10%. When the
rate is unknown, report hammer only and say the real cost is higher.
